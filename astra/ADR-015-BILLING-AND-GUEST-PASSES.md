## Guest-pass rules

A current paid member receives three creation credits per UTC calendar month, counted across that issuer's passes. Manual/complimentary entitlements and guest trials do not earn credits. Creation requires an active relationship, an enabled offer, positive guest capacity, an active/past-due nonblocked subscription and a paid period still in force. Cancellation at period end does not itself remove current paid access or credits.

Each pass uses a cryptographically generated 256-bit secret carried in a private URL fragment; the database stores only its SHA-256 hash. It expires 30 days after creation, is redeemable once and carries no cash value. No unused-credit rollover or credit restoration on revocation is implemented. Owner queries return pass IDs and status, never the old secret. Replace Link rotates the hash on the same owned, unused, unrevoked, unexpired pass: its previous link stops working, its original expiry and credit usage remain unchanged. The owner can create another pass while credits remain. These controls recover management after reload without pretending the old secret is recoverable.

Acceptance requires an authenticated new client with no previous coaching relationship and no earlier accepted guest pass. A sender cannot redeem their own pass. The sender must still have qualifying paid membership and the coach's enabled offer must have capacity. The transaction serializes workspace capacity, creates a real coaching relationship and a seven-day `guest_pass` service entitlement, and notifies the active workspace owner. The trial is card-free, nonrenewing and limited to once per account; the client chooses whether to subscribe afterward. It is not a friendship invitation or referral cash program.
